Violin 2 Practice Room

Recordings from our section rehearsals, broken into chunks you can loop. Pick a piece for the player, the part, and notes.

How to use it

  • Click a chunk — on the strip or in the list — and it loops until you stop it.
  • Set A / Set B for your own loop points when a chunk starts in the wrong place.
  • Speed takes it down to half without dropping the pitch, so it stays in tune while you work it up.
  • Count-in gives you four clicks before the loop comes round again, so you come in on time instead of scrambling.
  • Edit markers to rename chunks as you go.

Naming the chunks

Chunks start out numbered, because the recording doesn't tell us where the rehearsal marks fall. Rename them as you listen: hit Edit markers, stop when you reach something you recognise, and call it what Chris calls it — 11, the wolf, where we always fall apart.

Your names stay in your browser. If you want the whole section to have them, send them to Nick and they'll go up for everyone.

About the recordings

These were taken on a phone in the room, so they sound rough and a bit distant. The levels have been evened out so you can hear the quiet passages. The untouched originals are in the Drive folder.
