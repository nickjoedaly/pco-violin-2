Violin 2 Practice Room

Rehearsal recordings from our section, broken into chunks you can loop. Pick a piece to get the player, the score slot and the notes.

How to use this

  • Click a chunk — on the strip or in the list — and it loops that chunk until you stop it.
  • Set A / Set B mark your own loop points if a chunk boundary is in the wrong place.
  • Speed slows playback to 50% without dropping the pitch, so it stays in tune as you work it up.
  • Count-in gives you four clicks before the loop restarts, so you enter in time rather than scrambling.
  • Edit markers lets you rename chunks to real rehearsal letters or bar numbers as you listen.

About the chunk markers

The chunk boundaries were found automatically, by detecting where the texture of the recording changes. They are a scaffold, not rehearsal letters — the names are Chunk 1, Chunk 2 and so on because nothing in the audio says otherwise.

Rename them as you go. Open Edit markers, play until you reach a landmark you recognise, and name it — Letter C, m. 48, the wolf. Your names save in your browser straight away. To make them permanent for the whole section, copy the JSON the editor shows you into assets/data.js and commit it.

Recording quality

These came off a phone as 8 kHz mono voice-memo files, which is why they sound boxy. Levels have been evened out so the quiet passages are audible, but the bandwidth is what it is. The untouched originals are in the Drive folder.
